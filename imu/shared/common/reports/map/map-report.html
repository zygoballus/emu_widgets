• Map Report Quick Help

    To access map controls to do things like change underlying maps, set how points cluster etc
    Click the 'Map Controls' Link
    To minimise the map controls
    Click the associated 'up angle' icon at the top of the control
    To maximise the map controls
    Click the associated 'down angle' icon at the top of the control
    To move the map controls
    Click and drag the control
    To resize thelegend
    Click and drag the right and bottom borders of the legend
    To pan the map display
    Click the map and drag
    To zoom in or out
    any of the following actions can be used to zoom the map display
    • Click the +/- button on the map
    • Hold the Shift key down and click and drag a rectangle on the map
    • Move the mouse scroll wheel up or down
    • double clicking on the map will zoom in one level
    To get information on the items represented by a map marker
    Click the mouse on the marker
    To get more details on an item
    After selecting the marker, click on the wanted item summary
    To merge items within a certain distance into a single marker
    Open 'Map Controls' and adjust the 'cluster distance' setting
    To change the way the points are assigned a marker (eg to group by a different property or use different symbols etc)
    Open the Map Controls (or click on the configuration icon in the Legend) and adjust the display options
