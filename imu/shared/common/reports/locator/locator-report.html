  • Locator Report Quick Help

    To access map controls to adjust the map display
    Click the 'Map Controls' Menu Item. NB If wanted, this menu can be dragged away from the map.
    To minimise or minimise any controls
    Click the associated 'up' or 'down' icon at the top left of the control
    To pan the map display
    Click the map and drag
    To zoom in or out
    any of the following actions can be used to zoom the map display
    • Click the +/- button on the map
    • Hold the Shift key down and click and drag a rectangle on the map
    • Move the mouse scroll wheel up or down
    • double clicking on the map will zoom in one level
    To get information on items represented by a map marker
    Click the mouse on the marker. This will display all items that contribute items to the selected marker.
    To get more details on a particular item
    Click on the item in the selected marker list link
    To merge all items within a certain distance into a single marker
    Open 'Map Controls' and adjust the 'cluster distance' setting
    To display items by a different property
    Open the Map Controls (or click on the configuration icon in the Legend) and adjust the display options
